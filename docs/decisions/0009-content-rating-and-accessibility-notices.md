# ADR 0009: Content Boundaries and Player Warnings

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-31

## Decision

Galax Hero targets a teen-appropriate science-fiction action rating: stylized spacecraft combat, explosions, and fictional peril; no gore, sexual content, gambling, drugs, hate speech, or strong language. Narrative loss and imprisonment may be referenced without graphic detail.

The first-run flow and store page disclose flashing lights, rapid projectile patterns, screen shake, and online interactions if online play ships. Before gameplay, players can open accessibility settings and enable reduced flash, reduced motion, projectile outlines, simplified patterns, lower particle density, lower shake, game-speed assistance, auto-fire, and color-vision palettes.

Online text/voice chat is not included, avoiding an unmoderated communication channel. Player names supplied by a platform are escaped and length-limited. Any future chat, telemetry, or account feature requires a separate privacy, moderation, parental-control, and rating review.
