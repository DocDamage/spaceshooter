## Feature copy

Fight through six operations and sixty replayable stages. Master primary, secondary, and heavy weapons, spells, melee, shields, super modes, specialist ships, equipment sets, skills, and wingman commands. Chase routes, secrets, objectives, ranks, local scoreboards, deterministic daily/weekly challenges, boss practice, survival, endless, mutators, and a configurable training lab. Play solo or with one local co-pilot using distinct devices and profiles/guest progress.

Accessibility includes remapping, auto-fire, toggle focus/shield, aim and speed assistance, simplified patterns, reduced flash/motion/shake/particles, projectile/player outlines, color alternatives, subtitle controls, UI/text scaling, dynamic range, vibration controls, and pseudolocalization/RTL layout foundations. Shipping language at 1.0 remains English until human-reviewed translations are approved.

## Requirements and disclosures

Minimum: Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 64-bit; four hardware threads at 2.5 GHz; 8 GiB RAM; DirectX 11/OpenGL 3.3-class GPU with 1 GiB shared/dedicated memory; 2 GiB available storage; 1280×720; keyboard/mouse. Controller optional; two-player local co-op requires keyboard+controller or two controllers.

Photosensitivity: contains rapid projectile patterns, moving backgrounds, screen shake, and flashing combat effects; reduction options are available before play. Online co-op is not included in the 1.0 promise and must not appear in launch feature lists or labels. The standalone build works offline and does not automatically upload analytics or crash reports.
